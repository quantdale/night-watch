universe, whether excluded operations can be mechanically proven read-only and

authority. Improve the mechanically justified bug-hunting surface only; do not

## Required outcome

Produce a deterministic, privacy-safe exclusion-chain census for every current
source surface; audit the existing mutability/read-only authority; investigate
multiple real-source proof families; and admit a versioned proof family only if
it is bounded, exact, dependency-current, mechanically falsifiable,

campaign plans, dossiers, Control Center DTOs, or Git. Synthetic fixtures use
recognizable fake sentinels and assert zero leakage across every projection
boundary.

## Scope of proof investigation

Candidate families include exact direct pure handlers, bounded exact read-call
cones, known read-only declarations with mechanically proven transitive cones,
query-builder terminal reads, and framework/generated metadata only when
implementation-cross-checked. The campaign must explicitly reject naming,
HTTP-only, comments, inferred datastore behavior, "no write observed", fuzzy
resolution, and incomplete cones.

must distinguish newly read-only-proven, mechanically proven but not
projectable, newly projectable, newly Phase-24-eligible, and still-excluded
surfaces. Completion requires canonical and isolated validation, continuity
and project truth, a clean pushed `main`, and truthful safety counts.
