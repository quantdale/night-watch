## Why

W11 preserved a valid historical result but could not measure current-source
unknown-defect yield: its frozen subscribed provider failed before a valid
unknown-source investigation. The mature W7-W11 substrate is available, and
the owner has authorized a distinct successor wave that must measure the
current eight-repository universe without changing W11's result, leaking
historical truth, or laundering provider failure into zero yield.

## What Changes

- Open a governed W12 task and OpenSpec change while preserving W11 as frozen
  predecessor evidence.
- Declare and execute a provider-selection policy before probing, freezing the
  first valid provider before any W12 investigation result.
- Rebaseline all eight admitted repositories and record current-source,
  reproduction, and sibling-identity evidence without modifying siblings.
- Freeze a machine-readable W12 evaluation definition covering the broad run,
  eight repository-scoped runs, budgets, scope, stopping rules, admission,
  leakage, safety, and novelty classification; guard its fingerprint against
  mutation or widened resume.
- Execute the predeclared current-source campaign and preserve provider,
  investigation, reproduction, candidate, admission, and safety receipts.
- Derive coverage and yield mechanically, keeping investigation breadth,
  reproduction breadth, candidates, mechanical admissions, refusals, and
  provider-blocked runs distinct.
- Perform read-only novelty adjudication only after mechanical admission and
  report the evidence-bounded class without claiming organizational novelty.
- Reconcile Production Completion Group 12 and the governed task/project
  documentation, then validate, integrate, release, and report one truthful
  W12 verdict.
