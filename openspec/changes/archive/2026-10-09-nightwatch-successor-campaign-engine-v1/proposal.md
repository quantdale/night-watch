## Why

The certified baseline has a large, evidence-backed local backlog. This
successor programme first resolves the shard runner's reproduced misleading
green execution receipt, then reassesses and executes further local campaigns
without reopening the completed priority remediation campaign.

## What Changes

- Establish a fresh evidence-ranked successor backlog and campaign loop.
- Require mechanical reproduction before implementation selection.
- Track each selected campaign in its own child task and OpenSpec change.
- Preserve local/synthetic-only safety and exact C-00 integration boundaries.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `successor-campaign-engine`: Defines evidence-led local campaign selection,
  reproduction gates, checkpoint discipline, and successor reassessment.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Affected scope: `.agent` continuity, OpenSpec/task records, local validation,
  and selected Nightwatch source/test changes.
